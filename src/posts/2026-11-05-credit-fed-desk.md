Good morning. It's Thursday, November 5th. You're listening to the Credit and Fed Desk.

Wednesday was a big day across markets. The Dow jumped 3 percent, its best day since April of last year. Oil fell more than 8 percent. And the ten-year Treasury yield closed at 4.90 percent, down 5 basis points.

This morning added two more pieces. Jobless claims came in at 189,000, matching the low from July. And the Bank of England raised rates.

Our theme today is haves and have-nots. Meta came back to the bond market, this time in euros, and drew what Bloomberg Law calls a record order book for a European corporate bond. On the same day, CoreWeave's 2031 bonds traded as low as 79 and a half cents on the dollar. The biggest tech companies rallied. Chipmakers and the neoclouds got punished. And even the Fed has a new kind of "have" in the picture: a Senate that has to confirm whoever gets nominated.
