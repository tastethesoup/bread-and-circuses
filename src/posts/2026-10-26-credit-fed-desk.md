Good morning. It's Monday, October 26th, and you are listening to the Credit and Fed Desk.

The weekend brought three surprises.

First, Moody's did not cut France. It affirmed the rating and kept the negative outlook. On Friday I told you a cut was widely expected. That forecast was wrong.

Second, Brazil re-elected Lula, and markets did not see it coming. Before the New York open, a Brazil stock fund was down about 27 percent, and São Paulo's exchange halted trading.

Third, Meta, which bond investors spent last week worrying about, said this morning it will raise up to about 95 billion dollars in stock. Not bonds. And SpaceX signed a lease worth about 40 billion dollars to finance its chips.

So here's the theme. The front door to the junk-bond market is shut. The biggest borrowers are finding side doors. Everyone else is waiting outside.
