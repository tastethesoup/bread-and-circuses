Good morning. It's Tuesday, October 13th, and you are listening to the Credit and Fed Desk.

The bond market is back from the holiday, and it opened with a bid. The ten-year Treasury yield is trading around 5.19 to 5.20 percent this morning, per press and newsletter quotes. That's down about 4 basis points from Friday's official close of 5.24. Oil is lower, and that's the push.

The bigger story is at the Fed, where officials looked at the same AI boom and came back with different diagnoses. Governor Waller says the rise in yields is about real growth, not inflation. Chicago Fed President Goolsbee says AI is an inflation problem that is "jumping out of its lane." And this morning, Nick Timiraos flagged a line from Beth Hammack that sits awkwardly next to Chair Kevin Warsh's push to say less.

CPI is tomorrow at 7:30 Central. Futures put an October hike at roughly one in five to one in four.
