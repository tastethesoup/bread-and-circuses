Good morning. It's Wednesday, October 28th, and you are listening to the Credit and Fed Desk. It's Fed day. The decision lands at 1 PM Central, after this episode. So no predictions from me.

The story this morning is the buyers. Bloomberg's credit reporters talked to more than 30 people in data center financing. Raising cash, they say, is "significantly more difficult than it was just months ago." The big tech borrowers are paying the widest spreads of the whole AI buildout.

And yet. Two junk deals priced on Tuesday, and one of them got bigger. The five-year Treasury auction came in clean, with foreign and fund buyers back.

Loo, at State Street, put it best: the Treasury market "isn't losing foreign buyers, it's losing automatic buyers."

So here's the theme. Not gone, just choosier.
