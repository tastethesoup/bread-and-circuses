Good morning. It's Monday, November 2nd, and you are listening to the Credit and Fed Desk.

Friday was ugly for tech. Intel fell 28.58 percent, to 65 dollars and 76 cents. Per Bloomberg, that's its biggest drop in more than four decades. The Nasdaq fell 2.86 percent. The S and P 500 fell 1.82 percent.

Here's the line that sums it up. Joe Tigay at Equity Armor, quoted in Bloomberg's Friday evening newsletter, says Wall Street has moved from the "hopes and dreams phase" to the "show me" phase. That's his view, and it's our theme today. The show-me market.

Because the show-me demand didn't stop at stocks. This morning, Alphabet is back in the bond market, selling 6 billion euros of bonds. Demand is more than 17 billion euros. But it's paying up to get it done.

At the Fed, the speakers came out loud on Friday. Musalem said more firming will be necessary, "and soon." Logan said "at least two more" hikes. In other words: show us inflation coming down, or we keep going.

And oil is down about 6 percent this morning on hopes for diplomacy with Iran.
