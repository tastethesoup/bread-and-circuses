Good morning. It's Friday, November 6th. You're listening to the Credit and Fed Desk.

The October jobs report is out, and it was strong. Payrolls rose 165,000, against forecasts of roughly 59,000 to 65,000. The two months before it were revised up by 84,000 combined. Unemployment slipped to 4.1 percent. And wages rose 0.4 percent in the month.

That landed the morning after one of the worst days for tech stocks in years. On Thursday, the Nasdaq fell 5.52 percent, into what the wires call a correction. And per Joe Weisenthal on Bloomberg's Odd Lots, the VIX, Wall Street's fear gauge, hit its highest level since early April.

So our theme today is payrolls versus the tape. The jobs data say the Fed can keep leaning toward a hike. The AI trade just broke. A December hike is now roughly three in four, per secondary relays of CME FedWatch. And Nick Timiraos of the Wall Street Journal put that tension into a single post this morning.
