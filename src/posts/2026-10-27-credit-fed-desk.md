Good morning. It's Tuesday, October 27th, and you are listening to the Credit and Fed Desk. The Fed's two-day meeting starts today.

On Monday, Wall Street put together what the Wall Street Journal calls the largest AI debt deal in history: 63 billion dollars to finance chips for Anthropic. Broadcom's chief executive, Hock Tan, says it proves the capital is there.

He's right. But read the terms. Higher interest rates. A guarantee from Broadcom. A second lien from Blackstone. The same day, Anthropic's IPO prospectus warned it may need more money, sooner. And the one agency that rates it calls it junk.

So here's the theme. The money is there, at a price.
