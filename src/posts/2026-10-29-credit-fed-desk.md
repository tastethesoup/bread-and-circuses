Good morning. It's Thursday, October 29th, and you are listening to the Credit and Fed Desk. It's the day after the Fed, so this time we actually have a decision to talk about.

The Fed held on Wednesday. The vote was 10 to 2, and both dissenters wanted a hike. Then, this morning, the economy reported in. Third-quarter GDP grew 3.4 percent. September PCE inflation ran 3.6 percent from a year earlier. And jobless claims came in at 194,000, the lowest since mid-July.

Those headlines look strong. Credit spreads look calm. A junk deal priced inside its talk and got bigger.

But here's the line I keep coming back to. It's Warsh's, as reported by Nick Timiraos of the Wall Street Journal: "Interest rates get in the cracks."

So that's today's theme. In the cracks. The headline numbers are fine. The stress shows up in specific places: data center bonds, a shelved bond sale in Texas, the fine print on leveraged loans, and services inflation.
