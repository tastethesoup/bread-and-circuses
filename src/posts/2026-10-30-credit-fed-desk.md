Good morning. It's Friday, October 30th, and you are listening to the Credit and Fed Desk.

Alphabet had the worst day in its history on Thursday. The stock fell 12.6 percent. This morning, Amazon is down in premarket trading even after a record profit margin, and Intel is down more than 25 percent after a weak forecast.

And in the middle of all that, Microsoft went to the bond market and borrowed 25 billion dollars. According to Bloomberg, orders peaked around 115 billion.

Here's the line that sums it up. It comes from Jack Janasiewicz at Natixis, quoted by Bloomberg: "The market remains open for issuance, but not for everybody."

So that's today's theme. Open, but not for everybody. The bond market isn't shutting on the AI buildout. It's sorting the borrowers. The best names get mobbed. The rest pay up, and the lenders are starting to buy insurance.

On the Fed side, this morning's wage data came in about as expected, the odds of a December hike came down, and Treasuries rallied.
