Two records fell on Monday, and they were records that aren't supposed to go together. The ten-year Treasury yield broke through its 2007 high, the level from the bond selloff that helped set off the Global Financial Crisis. That takes it to the highest since 2002. And on the same day, the Nasdaq-100 rose 0.9 percent and topped 31,000 for the first time ever.

John Authers put it in his headline: "A Top-Heavy Stocks Rally Is Daring Bond Yields to Break It." This morning there's a little relief on the ten-year, but Monday handed the hawks an early argument.
