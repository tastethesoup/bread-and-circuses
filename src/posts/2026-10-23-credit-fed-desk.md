Good morning. It's Friday, October 23rd, and you are listening to the Credit and Fed Desk.

Thursday in two numbers.

First: bonds rated CCC and lower widened 33 basis points in a single day, to 13.40 percent. That's the widest in at least three years of the data I pull.

Second: the ten-year Treasury yield fell about 17 to 19 basis points, depending on the source, to just under 5 percent. Per Nick Timiraos of the Wall Street Journal, that was the biggest one-day drop since March 10th, 2023. The day Silicon Valley Bank failed.

Those two numbers are one story told from opposite ends. Money ran away from the riskiest corporate debt and straight into Treasuries. There's an old name for that: a flight to quality.
