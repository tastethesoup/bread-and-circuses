Good morning. It's Wednesday, October 14th, and you are listening to the Credit and Fed Desk.

September CPI is out, and the story is the core. Headline inflation rose 0.6 percent in the month and 3.7 percent from a year ago. That's roughly in line. Core inflation, which strips out food and energy, rose 0.3 percent in the month and 2.6 percent from a year ago. That's about a tenth of a point hotter than forecasters expected.

Yesterday, futures gave an October rate hike roughly a one-in-five to one-in-four chance. This morning, press relays put it in the mid-40s to about half. Nick Timiraos of the Wall Street Journal says the odds "moved up to 50 percent." Call it a coin flip.

The two-year Treasury yield jumped about 13 basis points in early trading, and the ten-year is near 5.30 percent.
