Week 1 is a noisy, incomplete picture, and we will treat it that way. The box scores still happened. So did the benches. A few managers already owe the group chat an apology.

## The games

**Paper Lions 142.8 - Idle Hands 117.3**

The Lions opened the year with a clean, slightly rude win. Ja'Marr Chase did the loud work. Idle Hands were close enough through Sunday afternoon, then watched a kicker and a defense finish the argument.

**Sunday Gentlemen 131.6 - The Commons 128.9**

Three points. One missed extra point. One leftover tight end on the bench. The Gentlemen survive. The Commons will replay the last two minutes of the app until Thursday.

**Bread Line 119.4 - Quiet Luxury 101.2**

Bread Line did not dazzle. They also did not leave starters in street clothes. Quiet Luxury started a running back who saw six touches and spent the rest of the afternoon as a decoy.

**Circus Maximus 156.1 - False Nine 110.8**

The first true blowout. Josh Allen and a stream of short scores made this look like a different sport. False Nine can burn the screenshot.

**Second Breakfast 124.0 - Harmless Fun 122.7**

A one-point game is a morality play. Harmless Fun left 14 points on the bench. Second Breakfast left 11. Both will claim they "knew." Neither sat the right guy.

**The Backbench 108.5 - Municipal Waste 97.6**

Ugly, low, and decided by whoever hated their quarterback less. Waste already has an injury in the backfield. Backbench already has a waiver claim typed in Notes.

## Crime blotter

- **The Commons** sat a tight end who outscored the starter by nine. That is the whole newsletter if you are in a hurry.
- **Harmless Fun** benched a wide receiver who saw 12 targets and then asked the chat whether waivers were "really necessary this early."
- **Quiet Luxury** started a defense on a short week against a bye-week backup. The defense scored 1.
- **Municipal Waste** announced a "wait and see" approach to an injured starter, then waited, then saw the backup go for 80 yards and a score on someone else's team.
- **False Nine** did not commit a crime so much as get robbed in daylight. Still, the empty flex spot at lock was a choice.

## Game of the Week

**Paper Lions vs Sunday Gentlemen**

The closest thing Week 2 has to a statement game. Both lineups look like they were built on purpose. Both managers will pretend they are not looking at the other's bench. If it stays within a field goal, we will call it a classic. If someone leaves a starter idle again, we will call it the season.
